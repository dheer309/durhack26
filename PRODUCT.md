# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Python, with a lightweight web dashboard. The brief suggests ReportLab, OpenCV, NumPy and Reed–Solomon.

## Users
A hackathon team demonstrating document provenance to judges.

## Product Purpose
Generate recipient-specific confidential PDFs and attempt to identify their recipient from a photographed page.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Use a deterministic physical watermark, error correction, and an explicit confidence score. Never return a guessed ID when decoding fails. Recipient details stay in a local database. Real-world print and photo reliability is unverified until measured.

## Evidence on Hand
The project brief is in AGENTS.md. No measured print or phone-photo results are available yet.
